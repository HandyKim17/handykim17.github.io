How It Protects Your Data via Gmail API

Antitheft uses the secure, official Google Gmail API to bridge the gap between your lost device and your personal safety. When a theft event is triggered, the app compiles the captured photos/videos and transmits them directly as an encrypted email payload to your own inbox.

1.

Direct Delivery: All evidence is sent using the gmail.send scope directly to your personal authenticated email box.

2.

No Middleman: We do not deploy any external database or third-party servers. Your tokens and media never bypass our secure application architecture.

3.

Full Sovereignty: You maintain absolute control over the OAuth consent permissions inside your Android settings panel at any time.
